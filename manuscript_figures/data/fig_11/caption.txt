Fig. 11. Voltage-derived base risk and multidimensional evidence support. a, Voltage base grades across the five complete-cell cohorts (98 vehicles). Colours encode low, medium and high risk; pattern overlays distinguish co-located recurrent support, distributed multidimensional support and cross-session auxiliary support. b, Evidence-state proportions within the 16 medium- and 13 high-risk vehicles. Unpatterned portions indicate vehicles lacking the specified repeated auxiliary support. c, Cross-tabulation against the reference labels normal, inconsistency and thermal runaway. Entries give vehicle counts and percentages within each reference-label row. d, Pooled vehicle-level label agreement when medium or high risk is a positive screen and inconsistency or thermal runaway is a positive reference label. The F1 score is the harmonic mean of precision and recall. Metrics pool all 98 vehicles from the reference and test cohorts.
