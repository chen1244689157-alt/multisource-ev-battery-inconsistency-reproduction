Fig. 9. VM-GMEFNet evidence fusion and candidate-cell ranking. a, Voltage-attention and mean auxiliary-evidence scores for the 38 training vehicles from platforms A and B, coloured by voltage base grade, with Normal denoting low risk. Both coordinates are dimensionless. b, Number of shared cells between the voltage-prior and network top-three candidate sets in five vehicles with identifiable relative SOC. Grey and green markers represent three-dimensional and four-dimensional evidence configurations, respectively; connecting lines pair the same vehicle. c, Learned auxiliary gate weights for those vehicles. The dimensionless gate weights quantify the network weighting of available auxiliary features. d, Voltage and network ranks for Cell 23, Cell 25 and Cell 47 in A-TR-01, followed by their voltage, equivalent resistance, module-temperature and relative-SOC values. Rank 1 denotes the highest rank. Dashed and dotted reference lines indicate −3 mV and zero relative-SOC offset, respectively.
