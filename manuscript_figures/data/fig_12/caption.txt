Fig. 12. Temporal evolution of inconsistency risk. a–c, Voltage scores recomputed from successive observation prefixes for A-TR-23, A-TR-02 and A-TR-01, respectively; panel titles state the final risk grade. Point colours show the grade at each evaluated prefix, and dashed and dotted lines indicate the domain-specific 75th- and 90th-percentile boundaries. d, Corresponding risk states for all 98 vehicles, grouped by cohort. Each row represents a vehicle and horizontal position is normalized to its own observation period. Grey denotes unavailable assessment or absent observations. Scores and grades are recalculated at each observation prefix. This retrospective reconstruction traces the emergence and evolution of risk evidence over the recorded observation period.
