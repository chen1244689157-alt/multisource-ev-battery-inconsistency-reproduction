Fig. 6. Multidimensional evidence and parameter identifiability for voltage candidates. a-c, Cell-wise evidence maps for A-TR-23, A-TR-02 and A-TR-01, respectively. Colours show evidence percentiles calculated separately within each vehicle and dimension, and red triangles and vertical lines mark the voltage-score-leading cell. Module temperatures are mapped to their associated cell indices. Grey regions indicate non-identifiable (NI) relative SOC. d, Candidate-associated values in the same vehicle order: low-side voltage offset in mV, dimensionless equivalent-resistance robust z score, module-relative temperature in °C and relative-SOC offset in percentage points (pp). e, Spatial relation of repeated auxiliary evidence to voltage candidates among 16 medium- and 13 high-risk vehicles. Labels give vehicle counts; bar widths give within-grade proportions. Co-located evidence overlaps the candidate cell or its module, whereas distributed evidence occurs elsewhere. f, Percentages of vehicles meeting each dimension’s identifiability criteria by cohort. Tr and Te denote training and test cohorts.
