Fig. 7. Auxiliary-evidence distributions and representative physical responses. a-c, Candidate-associated thermal support, relative equivalent-resistance response and relative SOC offset among identifiable medium- and high-risk vehicles. Each point is one vehicle; sample counts are given beneath each group. Boxes span the 25th-75th percentiles, central lines show medians and whiskers extend to the 5th–95th percentile limits; all vehicle observations are overlaid. The resistance axis uses a symmetric logarithmic scale to retain zero-valued responses. d, Fractions of eligible sessions supporting elevated module temperature in A-TR-06; red triangles mark voltage-candidate modules. e, Cell-wise fractions of supporting equivalent-resistance sessions in A-TR-01, with candidate cells marked. f, Its cell-wise relative-SOC offsets, highlighting Cell 23, Cell 25 and Cell 47. Dashed zero lines provide the no-offset or no-support reference. Dimension-specific identifiability requirements yield different sample sizes across dimensions.
