Fig. 10. VM-GMEFNet structural sensitivity and voltage-dominant stability. a, Mean rank correlation between network and voltage-prior ordering across 36 combinations of the auxiliary cap and voltage and auxiliary coefficients. b, Percentage of vehicles retaining the same leading candidate as the default configuration. Rows specify the auxiliary and voltage coefficients; columns specify the auxiliary cap. c, Mean auxiliary contribution versus the cap, with the range across coefficient combinations indicated by the error bars. The highlighted marker and vertical dashed line identify the default cap of 0.30. All panels use the same 38 training vehicles from platforms A and B. Candidate-rank agreement summarizes ranking stability, and auxiliary contribution quantifies the magnitude of the gated adjustment.
