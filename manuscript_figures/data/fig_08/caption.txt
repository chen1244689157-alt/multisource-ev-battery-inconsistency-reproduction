Fig. 8. Vehicle-adaptive OCV-SOC reference and module-temperature evolution. a, Stationary anchors (blue), SOC-bin medians (green) and the monotonic OCV-SOC reference (red) for A-TR-01. The fit uses 22 qualified anchors spanning 47 SOC percentage points; grey reference segments identify slopes below the inversion threshold of 0.8 mV per SOC percentage point. b, Stationary voltage departures from the fitted reference for candidate Cell 23, Cell 25 and Cell 47, with the pack-median departure shown in grey. c,d, Session-level 95th-percentile module-relative temperatures over elapsed time in A-TR-06 and A-TR-01, respectively. Module labels identify candidate, neighbouring or supporting locations; M denotes module index. The two cases contrast a localized thermal region near the candidates with evidence distributed across other modules. Temperature provides module-level context, while the stationary-voltage reference supports relative-SOC interpretation at cell level.
