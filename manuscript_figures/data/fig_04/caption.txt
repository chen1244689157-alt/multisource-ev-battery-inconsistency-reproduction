Fig. 4. Cell-voltage behaviour and operating conditions of representative risk grades. Columns show A-TR-23, A-TR-02 and A-TR-01, representing low (labelled Normal), medium and high risk. a-c, Relative voltage of the score-leading cell (red) against the contemporaneous 10th-90th percentile range of the remaining cells (blue). The horizontal dashed line marks -3 mV. Shaded time regions identify the first and last 20% of valid snapshots used for stage comparison. d-f, Median relative-voltage profiles across cell indices in the early (green) and late (purple) stages; highlighted markers identify the score-leading cell. The reported changes refer to that cell. g-i, State of charge (SOC; left axis) and pack current (right axis) over the same observation period. Vehicle codes combine the domain, training (TR) or test (TE) split, and an anonymized within-group index. The three cases link risk grades to cell behaviour and its operating context.
