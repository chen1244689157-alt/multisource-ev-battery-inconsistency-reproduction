Fig. 13. Brand-wise conditional-response modelling and pack-level screening of aggregated charging segments. a, Vehicle scores divided by their brand-specific calibrated thresholds and ranked within each brand (n = 198, 49 and 100). The dashed line at one is the screening boundary. b, Accuracy, specificity and false-positive rate against the source-provided vehicle-level normal and abnormal labels across all 347 vehicles from five-fold vehicle-isolated cross-validation (CV); displayed values are rounded to whole percentages. c,d, Observed session voltage-spread 95th percentiles against median SOC for vehicle 100 (431 sessions) and vehicle 107 (42 sessions). Each point represents a session, coloured by charging current. e,f, Corresponding session 5th-percentile minimum-cell voltage and 95th-percentile temperature spread, with blue circles and coral diamonds distinguishing the two vehicles. These measured session summaries characterize the voltage and thermal responses associated with the vehicle screening scores.
