Fig. 1. Voltage-led battery inconsistency screening and multidimensional explainable localization. a, Multisource cell-voltage records from datasets A-C undergo time ordering, session reconstruction and quality control to generate native-resolution records and 10-min snapshots. b, Physical evidence comprises module-relative temperature, equivalent-resistance step responses and conditionally identifiable relative SOC. c, Relative-voltage stage changes and terminal deviations determine risk grades, while condition-matched historical references characterize cross-session recurrence. d, VM-GMEFNet integrates voltage priors with availability-masked auxiliary evidence for candidate ranking and evidence verification. e, Hierarchical outputs include voltage risk, candidate locations, evidence support and retrospective timing. f, The independent dataset-D path uses DOC-MLP to model brand-specific normal charging responses and aggregates the highest-scoring 5% of sessions for pack-level screening.
