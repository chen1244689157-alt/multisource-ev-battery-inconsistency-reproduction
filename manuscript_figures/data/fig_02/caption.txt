Fig. 2. Cohort scale, operating coverage and quality control. a, Vehicle numbers and observation resolution; the horizontal axis is logarithmic. A and B contain separate training and test cohorts, C is an independent source domain, and D provides pack-level charging records. b, Contours enclosing approximately 75% of the observation distribution in SOC–absolute-current space for each domain. c, Principal component analysis (PCA) of vehicle-level operating profiles; each point represents one vehicle from A training, B training, C or D. Profiles comprise the median and interquartile range of SOC, absolute current, upper-temperature statistics and voltage spread. The first two principal components (PC1 and PC2) explain 46.8% and 18.9% of the variance, respectively. d, Retention after quality control, with analysis counts shown as records for A-C and sessions for D. The dashed line indicates complete retention.
