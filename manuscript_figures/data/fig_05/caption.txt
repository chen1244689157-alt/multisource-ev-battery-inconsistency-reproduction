Fig. 5. Condition-matched historical reference and cross-session recurrence. Columns compare low-risk A-TR-23 (labelled Normal; Cell 2), medium-risk A-TR-02 (Cell 26) and high-risk A-TR-01 (Cell 25). a-c, Session-level conditional-residual maps across cell indices and chronologically ordered eligible sessions; colour indicates the median voltage departure of each cell in each session. d-f, Relative-voltage profiles summarized in SOC bins for condition-matched early references (blue circles) and supported late observations (red diamonds). Blue shading shows the interquartile range of the matched historical references. Early and late sets contain the first and last 20% of valid snapshots. g-i, Candidate-cell session residuals (left axis) and negative-session fractions over the latest 3, 6 and 12 eligible sessions (right axis). A negative session has at least three valid residuals, with at least half below -3 mV.
