Fig. 3. Voltage-derived risk distributions across full-cell cohorts. a-e, Ascending vehicle voltage scores for A training (n = 25), B training (n = 13), A test (n = 30), B test (n = 20) and C (n = 10), respectively. Each point represents one vehicle; blue, green and coral indicate low (labelled Normal in the legend), medium and high risk. Dashed and dotted lines mark the 75th- and 90th-percentile reference boundaries. The test cohorts of platforms A and B use boundaries frozen from their respective training cohorts; C uses its own reference distribution. The score is the product of a voltage change and a dimensionless terminal-deviation fraction and thus retains millivolt units. f, Cohort-wise vehicle counts in each grade across all 98 vehicles.
